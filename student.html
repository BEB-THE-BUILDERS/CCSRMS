About CCSRMS
College of Computer Studies Room Management System

CCSRMS is the room scheduling system of the College of Computer Studies at Gordon College. As a student, you can use it to see which rooms are used, by which section and professor, and at what time, from Monday to Saturday. Schedules are managed by the department head and the program coordinators, so what you see here is the latest version they have set. Your view is read only. If something looks wrong, tell your program coordinator.

Terms and Conditions

Please read carefully and scroll to the bottom to accept before using CCSRMS.

1. Introduction

Welcome to the CCS Room Management System (CCSRMS), a web-based platform of the College of Computer Studies (CCS) at Gordon College. By using this system, you agree to the terms and conditions in this document.

2. Purpose of the System

CCSRMS is used to manage and schedule classrooms and laboratory rooms of the College of Computer Studies. As a student, you can:

  • View which rooms are used, and at what time, from Monday to Saturday
  • See the section, professor, and subject type (Major or Minor, Lecture or Laboratory) of each class
  • Filter the schedule by program (BSCS, BSIT, BSEMC-DAT, BSEMC-GD)

Student accounts are view only. You cannot create, edit, move, or delete any schedule.

3. Who Can Use the System

CCSRMS is for currently enrolled CCS students. Your account is tied to your student ID number and your program. Your program is set when you sign up and cannot be changed afterward. Do not create an account using someone else's ID number.

4. User Responsibilities

As a user, you agree to:

  • Use the system only to check room schedules
  • Give correct information when you sign up
  • Not share the schedule data with others in a way that misleads them
  • Report wrong or missing schedules to your program coordinator or the department head

5. Account Security

You are responsible for your login credentials. You agree to:

  • Keep your password private and not share it with anyone
  • Log out after each session, especially on shared or public devices
  • Change your password using the Change Password option in the sidebar if you think someone else knows it

The CCS is not responsible for problems caused by a password that you shared or did not keep safe.

6. Data and Privacy

CCSRMS stores the following about your account:

  • Your school email (built from your student ID number) and your encrypted password
  • Your program (BSCS, BSIT, BSEMC-DAT, or BSEMC-GD)
  • Your acceptance of these terms

This data is used only for running the system and is not shared with outside parties. The room schedules in CCSRMS belong to the College of Computer Studies.

7. Prohibited Actions

The following may lead to account suspension or school disciplinary action:

  • Trying to use another person's account
  • Trying to change, delete, or access schedule data you are not allowed to
  • Trying to hack, overload, or reverse-engineer the system
  • Creating an account with false information

8. System Availability and Changes

CCSRMS is provided as available. The administrators may pause the system for maintenance, change or remove features, or update these terms at any time. Changes take effect the next time you log in. If you keep using CCSRMS after a change, you accept the updated terms.

9. Disclaimer

Schedules can change during the term. CCSRMS shows the latest information entered by the department, but it may not always match last-minute changes. Please confirm with your professor or program coordinator if you are unsure. The College of Computer Studies and the developers of CCSRMS are not liable for missed classes or other problems caused by relying only on this system.
